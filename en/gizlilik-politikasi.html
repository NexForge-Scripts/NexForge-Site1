Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

At NexForge Scripts, we value your privacy. This privacy policy explains what information is collected when you visit our website or use our contact form, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Our website only collects personal data in the following situations:

  • Contact Form: When you send us your email address and message, this information is used solely to respond to you.
  • Server Logs: Our web server may record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data cannot be used to personally identify you.

2. How We Use Your Data

The collected information is only used for the following purposes:

  • Responding to inquiries submitted through the contact form
  • Improving the website and enhancing user experience
  • Complying with legal obligations

Your email address and message are never shared, sold, or rented to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Contact form messages are transmitted via Formspree and forwarded to our email inbox. Messages are retained as long as necessary for communication purposes. They can be deleted upon your request.

4. Cookies

This website does not use cookies. No tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies are present.

5. Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to third-party services such as GitHub, Discord, and Buy Me a Coffee. These sites have their own privacy policies, and NexForge Scripts is not responsible for their data processing practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your personal data. Our website is served over HTTPS, and contact form data is transmitted encrypted.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to the processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at nexforgescripts@gmail.com.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy:

Email: nexforgescripts@gmail.com

9. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary. Changes take effect as soon as they are published on this page.
