Features

Advanced Moderation

Automatic filters, spam protection, warning system and custom moderation commands. Protect your server against malicious users.

Music Player

High-quality music streaming with YouTube, SoundCloud and Spotify support. Playlists, queue management and volume control.

Games & Fun

Mini-games, contests and interactive commands to keep your server lively. Help your members have a great time.

Detailed server statistics, message logs, member tracking and reporting. Keep your finger on the pulse of your server.
